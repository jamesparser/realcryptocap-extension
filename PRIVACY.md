# RealCryptoCap browser extension: privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

RealCryptoCap (the "extension") shows a bar with the top 50 cryptocurrencies by real market cap, news headlines and an AI chat on pages you choose.

## What the extension does with data

- **Page access.** The extension only runs on a page after you click its toolbar icon. It adds its own bar to that page. It does not read, collect or send the content of the pages you visit.
- **Settings.** Your choices (display mode, header or footer, ticker speed) are stored on your device with Chrome's storage. They are never sent anywhere.
- **Market data.** To show prices, headlines and market stats, the extension requests public data from realcryptocap.com. These requests carry no account and no personal information.
- **AI chat.** When you send a message in the AI chat, the extension sends your question, your last few chat messages in that session, and the market data currently shown in the bar to the RealCryptoCap AI service at realcryptocap.com, so it can answer. Do not type personal or sensitive information into the chat. The service counts questions per day to enforce the free daily limit, which uses your IP address and browser type. We do not sell this data and do not use it for advertising.
- **Daily question counter.** A counter of the AI questions you asked today is kept on your device.

## What the extension does not do

No analytics or tracking scripts, no advertising, no sale or sharing of data with third parties, no remote code. All code that runs is included in the extension package.
